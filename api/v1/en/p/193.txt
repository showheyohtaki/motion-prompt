Motion Prompt #193

What it is: A pale painting with soft bleeds and white paper showing
What the sample shows: The line drawing of a house, a tree and the sun becomes a pale transparent watercolor, with colors bleeding and pooling and the white paper left showing.
Spec:
- Medium: transparent watercolor on paper
- Look: colors that bleed and pool, white paper left unpainted, pale colors overall
- Sample: the same base sketch (a line drawing of a house, a tree and the sun on a hill) was given to an image model as a reference and redrawn in this style. A diagonal wipe turns the sketch into the style from 0.5 s to 1.35 s, the picture slowly zooms to 1.045x until 4.0 s, and it fades back to the sketch from 4.0 s to 4.5 s (one pass = 4.7 s, cubic ease-in-out)
When to use it: For picture books, illustrations or seasonal cards that should feel soft and hand-painted.
