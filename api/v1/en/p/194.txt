Please use "Impasto oil painting" (油絵（厚塗り）), one of the names in "Art styles for image generation".
Motion Prompt #194

What it is: Thick paint with visible brush and knife marks
What the sample shows: The same line drawing becomes a richly colored oil painting with thick paint; brush strokes and knife marks stand out as ridges.
Spec:
- Medium: oil paint laid on thickly (impasto)
- Look: brush strokes and palette-knife marks stay as raised ridges; strong, dense colors
- Sample: the same base sketch (a line drawing of a house, a tree and the sun on a hill) was given to an image model as a reference and redrawn in this style. A diagonal wipe turns the sketch into the style from 0.5 s to 1.35 s, the picture slowly zooms to 1.045x until 4.0 s, and it fades back to the sketch from 4.0 s to 4.5 s (one pass = 4.7 s, cubic ease-in-out)
When to use it: For posters and art pieces that should feel rich, heavy and hand-made.
