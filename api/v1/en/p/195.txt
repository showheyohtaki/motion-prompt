Please use "Ink wash painting (sumi-e)" (水墨画), one of the names in "Art styles for image generation".
Motion Prompt #195

What it is: Painted only with shades and washes of black ink
What the sample shows: The same line drawing becomes a picture in one black ink on washi paper, shaped only by dark and pale ink, bleeds and soft washes.
Spec:
- Medium: one black ink on washi paper
- Look: drawn only with dark and pale ink, bleeds and soft washes; no color
- Sample: the same base sketch (a line drawing of a house, a tree and the sun on a hill) was given to an image model as a reference and redrawn in this style. A diagonal wipe turns the sketch into the style from 0.5 s to 1.35 s, the picture slowly zooms to 1.045x until 4.0 s, and it fades back to the sketch from 4.0 s to 4.5 s (one pass = 4.7 s, cubic ease-in-out)
When to use it: For a quiet, Japanese feel with plenty of empty space.
