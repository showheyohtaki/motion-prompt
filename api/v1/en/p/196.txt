Please use "Ukiyo-e woodblock print" (浮世絵), one of the names in "Art styles for image generation".
Motion Prompt #196

What it is: A woodblock print with outlines and flat colors
What the sample shows: The same line drawing becomes an Edo-period color woodblock print: the house turns into a tiled-roof Japanese house, the tree into a pine, the sun into a red circle, and the sky fades from deep indigo at the top.
Spec:
- Medium: Edo-period multi-color woodblock print
- Look: thin, even black outlines, flat areas of color, slight misregistration between blocks, washi fibers and uneven printing
- Sky: graded from deep indigo at the top to pale below, with a band of mist across it; the sun as a red circle (the sample leaves out Mount Fuji, waves, people and text)
- Sample: the same base sketch (a line drawing of a house, a tree and the sun on a hill) was given to an image model as a reference and redrawn in this style. A diagonal wipe turns the sketch into the style from 0.5 s to 1.35 s, the picture slowly zooms to 1.045x until 4.0 s, and it fades back to the sketch from 4.0 s to 4.5 s (one pass = 4.7 s, cubic ease-in-out)
When to use it: For Japanese subjects shown boldly with flat color and line.
