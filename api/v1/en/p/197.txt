Please use "Pointillism" (点描), one of the names in "Art styles for image generation".
Motion Prompt #197

What it is: Painted with small dots of color placed side by side
What the sample shows: The same line drawing becomes a picture made only of dots of color; sky, house, tree and ground are all built from clearly visible, fairly large dots.
Spec:
- Technique: only dots of color placed side by side; no lines or filled areas
- Dot size: large enough to stay visible on a small card (tiny dots read as watercolor)
- Sample: the same base sketch (a line drawing of a house, a tree and the sun on a hill) was given to an image model as a reference and redrawn in this style. A diagonal wipe turns the sketch into the style from 0.5 s to 1.35 s, the picture slowly zooms to 1.045x until 4.0 s, and it fades back to the sketch from 4.0 s to 4.5 s (one pass = 4.7 s, cubic ease-in-out)
When to use it: For a bright picture with a hand-made, rhythmic texture.

Reference implementation (Canvas 2D, a function whose picture depends only on the time): https://showheyohtaki.github.io/motion-prompt/api/v1/en/e/197.txt
