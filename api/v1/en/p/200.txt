Please use "Stained glass" (ステンドグラス), one of the names in "Art styles for image generation".
Motion Prompt #200

What it is: Glowing colored glass divided by black lead lines
What the sample shows: The same line drawing becomes a glass picture divided by black lead lines; sky, hill, house and tree each turn into pieces of colored glass with light shining through.
Spec:
- Material: pieces of colored glass joined by lead, as in a window
- Look: areas of color divided by thick black lines, glowing as light passes through
- Sample: the same base sketch (a line drawing of a house, a tree and the sun on a hill) was given to an image model as a reference and redrawn in this style. A diagonal wipe turns the sketch into the style from 0.5 s to 1.35 s, the picture slowly zooms to 1.045x until 4.0 s, and it fades back to the sketch from 4.0 s to 4.5 s (one pass = 4.7 s, cubic ease-in-out) (this sample is cropped slightly wider so the lead lines of the hill show)
When to use it: For a solemn or festive decorative look that glows with light.
