Please use "Neon sign" (ネオンサイン), one of the names in "Art styles for image generation".
Motion Prompt #202

What it is: Shapes drawn with glowing tubes on a dark wall
What the sample shows: The same line drawing becomes glowing neon tubes on a dark brick wall; the house, tree, sun and the line of the hill glow and bloom.
Spec:
- Material: a dark brick wall and glowing neon tubes
- Look: shapes drawn only with tube lines, with the light blooming around them; the sample uses red, yellow and green tubes
- Sample: the same base sketch (a line drawing of a house, a tree and the sun on a hill) was given to an image model as a reference and redrawn in this style. A diagonal wipe turns the sketch into the style from 0.5 s to 1.35 s, the picture slowly zooms to 1.045x until 4.0 s, and it fades back to the sketch from 4.0 s to 4.5 s (one pass = 4.7 s, cubic ease-in-out)
When to use it: For a night-time mood or a shop or bar sign look.

Reference implementation (Canvas 2D, a function whose picture depends only on the time): https://showheyohtaki.github.io/motion-prompt/api/v1/en/e/202.txt
Shared helper code: https://showheyohtaki.github.io/motion-prompt/api/v1/kit/artstyle.js
