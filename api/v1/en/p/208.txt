Please use "Line art" (ラインアート), one of the names in "Art styles for image generation".
Motion Prompt #208

What it is: Drawn only with lines in one color, no fill
What the sample shows: The same line drawing becomes a detailed drawing in one dark navy pen on cream paper, with every roof tile, leaf, blade of grass and sun ray drawn in line.
Spec:
- Medium: one dark navy pen on cream paper
- Look: fine lines only, with every detail drawn in; the line weight varies
- Avoid: fills and shaded areas
- Sample: the same base sketch (a line drawing of a house, a tree and the sun on a hill) was given to an image model as a reference and redrawn in this style. A diagonal wipe turns the sketch into the style from 0.5 s to 1.35 s, the picture slowly zooms to 1.045x until 4.0 s, and it fades back to the sketch from 4.0 s to 4.5 s (one pass = 4.7 s, cubic ease-in-out)
When to use it: For coloring pages, field guides or packaging where the beauty of the line should show.

Reference implementation (Canvas 2D, a function whose picture depends only on the time): https://showheyohtaki.github.io/motion-prompt/api/v1/en/e/208.txt
Shared helper code: https://showheyohtaki.github.io/motion-prompt/api/v1/kit/artstyle.js
