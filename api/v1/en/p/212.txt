Please use "Papercraft" (ペーパークラフト), one of the names in "Art styles for image generation".
Motion Prompt #212

What it is: Paper folded and glued into 3D models
What the sample shows: The same line drawing becomes a photo of models folded from colored card stock: the house is a box, the tree and sun are polyhedra, and folds, glue tabs and cut edges show.
Spec:
- Build: 3D models of colored card stock, cut from a template, folded and glued
- Look: crisp fold edges, small glue tabs sticking out, white cut edges of the paper; a photo taken on a desk from the front at an angle
- Note: not a white-only relief, and not the flat stacked layers of Layered paper art (#206)
- Sample: the same base sketch (a line drawing of a house, a tree and the sun on a hill) was given to an image model as a reference and redrawn in this style. A diagonal wipe turns the sketch into the style from 0.5 s to 1.35 s, the picture slowly zooms to 1.045x until 4.0 s, and it fades back to the sketch from 4.0 s to 4.5 s (one pass = 4.7 s, cubic ease-in-out)
When to use it: For hand-made 3D product images, or to show crafts and teaching materials.

Reference implementation (Canvas 2D, a function whose picture depends only on the time): https://showheyohtaki.github.io/motion-prompt/api/v1/en/e/212.txt
Shared helper code: https://showheyohtaki.github.io/motion-prompt/api/v1/kit/artstyle.js
