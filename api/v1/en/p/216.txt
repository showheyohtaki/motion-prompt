Please use "Blueprint" (ブループリント（設計図風）), one of the names in "Art styles for image generation".
Motion Prompt #216

What it is: A technical drawing in white lines on blue
What the sample shows: The same line drawing becomes a technical drawing in fine white lines on blue, with a few dimension and construction lines.
Spec:
- Look: a drawing in fine white lines on a blue ground
- Lines: a few dimension and construction lines (the sample has no numbers or text)
- Sample: the same base sketch (a line drawing of a house, a tree and the sun on a hill) was given to an image model as a reference and redrawn in this style. A diagonal wipe turns the sketch into the style from 0.5 s to 1.35 s, the picture slowly zooms to 1.045x until 4.0 s, and it fades back to the sketch from 4.0 s to 4.5 s (one pass = 4.7 s, cubic ease-in-out)
When to use it: To show how a product works, or to give a "being planned" feel.

Reference implementation (Canvas 2D, a function whose picture depends only on the time): https://showheyohtaki.github.io/motion-prompt/api/v1/en/e/216.txt
Shared helper code: https://showheyohtaki.github.io/motion-prompt/api/v1/kit/artstyle.js
