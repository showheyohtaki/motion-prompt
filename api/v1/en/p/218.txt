Motion Prompt #218

What it is: Two axes that split items into four quadrants
What the sample shows: Two axes grow out from the center, four areas appear and dots are placed; only the top-right area and its dot are blue.
Spec:
- Axes: grow out from the center from 0.4 s to 1.0 s (168 px to each side, 86 px up and down, cubic ease-in-out), with arrowheads at the right and top ends
- Areas: four 154×78 px boxes with 10 px corners, 8 px from the axes; they fade in over 0.35 s in the order top-right, top-left, bottom-left, bottom-right, starting at 1.0 s, 0.08 s apart. Top-right is light blue, the rest light gray
- Dots: seven dots from 1.45 s, 0.1 s apart, each popping in over 0.3 s (ease-out-back, a slight overshoot); the one in the top-right is blue (radius 12 px), the others gray (radius 8 px)
- End: the whole figure fades out from 4.1 s to 4.5 s and the card stays empty until the 4.7 s loop
When to use it: To sort options by two criteria and point to the one to aim for (top right).
