Motion Prompt #223

What it is: Arrows in a ring that keeps going round
What the sample shows: Circles numbered 1 to 4 sit on a ring, arrows draw one after another until the ring closes, then a blue highlight goes round once.
Spec:
- Layout: four light-blue circles (radius 26 px, numbered 1–4) at the top, right, bottom and left of an ellipse 128 px wide and 70 px tall (radii), popping in from 0.4 s, 0.32 s apart
- Arrows: arcs along the ellipse from each circle to the next (mid blue, 3 px, 13 px heads), drawn over 0.28 s from 0.7 s, 0.32 s apart; the ring closes at 1.94 s
- Highlight: from 2.2 s to 3.8 s a solid blue fill moves 1 → 2 → 3 → 4, 0.4 s per step, with soft edges
- End: the whole figure fades out from 4.1 s to 4.5 s and the card stays empty until the 4.7 s loop
When to use it: To show a process that returns to the start and keeps repeating, such as PDCA.
