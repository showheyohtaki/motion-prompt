Motion Prompt #229

What it is: A half-circle dial and needle that show progress
What the sample shows: The needle of a half-circle dial swings past, wobbles and settles at 72%; the blue band and the number follow the needle.
Spec:
- Dial: a half-circle band of radius 120 px and 30 px thick (gray), with ticks every 10% (longer at 0, 50 and 100%)
- Needle: starts at 0.5 s and follows a damped spring 1 − e^(−3.2t)·cos(7.5t), swinging past and wobbling before it settles at 72%
- Band and number: the band is blue up to the needle, and the percentage (26 px) below matches the needle
- End: from 4.1 s to 4.5 s the needle, band and number go back to 0% (cubic ease-in-out; one pass = 4.7 s)
When to use it: To show with one number how far you are toward a target.
