Motion Prompt #234

What it is: Events in order along one line
What the sample shows: One line is drawn from left to right; as its tip passes each point, a dot and a month label appear, alternating above and below. The last one is blue.
Spec:
- Line: from x = 36 to x = 503 at y = 106 (dark gray, 3 px), drawn from 0.4 s to 2.0 s (cubic ease-in-out), with an arrowhead at the right end
- Dots: five (April, June, August, October, December: 4月 … 12月), each popping in over 0.25 s at the moment the tip reaches it (radius 7 px; the last one blue, 10 px)
- Labels: from each dot a 22 px stem grows up or down, alternating, to a 64×32 px rounded tag (white with the month in 18 px; the last one blue with white text), over 0.3 s
- End: the whole figure fades out from 4.1 s to 4.5 s and the card stays empty until the 4.7 s loop
When to use it: To show events in time order: a history, how something came about, or a plan.
