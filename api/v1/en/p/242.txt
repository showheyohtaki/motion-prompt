Motion Prompt #242

What it is: The product turns as you scroll
What the sample shows: Scrolling turns a lemon soda can and scrolling back turns it the other way. The motion is a swap between 36 frames; the frame number and a progress line show where it is.
Spec:
- A 511 × 170 viewport: product copy and a 150 px progress line on the left, a can (radius 36, height 118) on the right. One pass takes 4.7 s (twice per 9.4 s pack cycle).
- Scroll s: 0→1 over 0.3–2.3 s, 1→0 over 2.9–4.4 s (cubic ease-in-out). Frame f = floor(s × 36): 36 frames per turn, 10° per frame.
- No interpolation between frames: the picture only changes when f changes, so slow scrolling reads as stepping through frames.
- The can's label, dots and lettering sit at longitudes φ: x = 36·sin(φ + f × 10°), shown only on the side where cos is positive and squeezed by cos. Shading on both edges.
- A "frame 004 / 36" readout and the progress line follow f; the scrollbar on the right follows the scroll.
When to use it: Use it to show a product all the way round, or to let the scroll drive a video-like sequence as on Apple product pages.
