Please use "Shrinking header" (縮むヘッダー), one of the names in "Landing page & web effects, part 3".
Motion Prompt #245

What it is: On scroll it shrinks into a slim floating pill
What the sample shows: Scrolling down shrinks the full-width white header into a slim, rounded black bar floating at the top; the cart button loses its label and keeps only the icon. Scrolling back up restores it.
Spec:
- A 511 × 170 viewport. One pass takes 4.7 s (twice per 9.4 s pack cycle). Scroll: 0→150 px over 0.4–1.9 s, 150→0 over 2.9–4.3 s (cubic ease-in-out).
- Shrink amount q = easeInOut(scroll / 70): fully shrunk after 70 px, then it stays.
- With q: width 511 → 310, height 46 → 34, top gap 0 → 10, radius 0 → 17, color white → black (#1E1E21 at 92%), shadow blur 6 → 16; text 13 → 11 px, nav spacing 56 → 40 px.
- The cart button goes from 82 to 30 px wide: the label fades out and a bag icon fades in. Text color goes from black to white.
- #075 Sticky header only stays on top and #149 Hide-on-scroll header hides on the way down; a shrinking header stays and changes its shape and size.
When to use it: Use it when a large hero should have room at first, but the navigation should stay at hand in a compact form.
