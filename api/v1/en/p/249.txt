Motion Prompt #249

What it is: Icons grow the closer the cursor gets
What the sample shows: As the cursor sweeps across seven icons along the bottom, the nearest ones grow and rise and the shelf stretches with them. The largest icon shows its name.
Spec:
- A 511 × 170 viewport: seven 34 × 34 icons (radius 24%) 8 px apart on a translucent white shelf. One pass takes 4.7 s (twice per 9.4 s pack cycle).
- Cursor: enters from the right over 0–0.5 s, x = 470 → 110 over 0.5–2.2 s, 110 → 470 over 2.6–4.2 s, out over 4.2–4.7 s (cubic ease-in-out).
- Scale = 1 + 0.85·exp(−(d/46)²) × in, where d is the horizontal distance from the icon's rest center to the cursor and in goes 0→1 over 0.35–0.65 s and 1→0 over 4.15–4.45 s.
- Icons are laid out again at their new sizes, so neighbors spread and the shelf widens; bottoms stay aligned and the icons grow upward.
- Above an icon past 1.55× a dark label shows its name.
When to use it: Use it for a row of app or section links that should be fun to sweep across.
