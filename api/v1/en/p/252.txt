Motion Prompt #252

What it is: The cursor stirs up swirling colored smoke
What the sample shows: Moving the cursor over a pale page releases smoke in several shades of blue along its path, which swirls, spreads and fades.
Spec:
- A 511 × 170 viewport on a pale blue ground (#F1F5FF). One pass takes 4.7 s (twice per 9.4 s pack cycle). The picture is computed on a 128 × 43 grid and stretched smoothly.
- The cursor moves in a figure eight: x = 255 + 170·sin(2πt/4.7), y = 86 + 48·sin(4πt/4.7). Every 0.1175 s a smoke puff is released there, cycling through blue, mid blue, blue, light blue and mid blue.
- Puffs live 1.4 s: radius 12 + 30 × age, strength exp(−2.1 × age) (0 → 1 over the first 0.1 s). Their centers drift outward in a spiral at 26 px/s and rise at 10 px/s.
- Each cell sums the weights e = strength × exp(−distance² / radius²) and moves from the ground color toward the weighted average color of the puffs by min(1, Σe × 1.6). Cell coordinates are warped with sin and cos (7 px and 6 px) to make it flow.
- A real fluid simulation solves a velocity field every frame; this sample replaces it with a function of time. #013 Liquid motion is a shape moving softly; here colored smoke mixes.
When to use it: Use it on music, art or tech landing pages where touching the hero should be the fun part.
