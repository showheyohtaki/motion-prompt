Motion Prompt #255

What it is: Letters flip over like a departure board
What the sample shows: Eleven letter tiles on a black board split and flip, passing through a few letters on the way, to change TOKYO 09:30 → PARIS 11:05 → SEOUL 13:40.
Spec:
- A 511 × 170 viewport: eleven 34 × 54 letter tiles (radius 5) 4 px apart. One pass takes 4.7 s (twice per 9.4 s pack cycle).
- Changes start at 0.5, 2.0 and 3.5 s (TOKYO 09:30 → PARIS 11:05 → SEOUL 13:40 → back). Letter i starts i × 0.045 s later.
- Each flip takes 0.09 s. Letters that stay the same do not move; others flip 3 to 5 times through intermediate letters and stop on the new one.
- One flip f (0–1): behind, the top half shows the next letter and the bottom half the current one. While f < 0.5 the current top half folds down by scaling vertically around the middle (darkening); from 0.5 the next bottom half unfolds downward.
- #156 Text roll on hover rolls letters over and #129 Text scramble swaps random letters; a split-flap display shows the split tiles physically falling.
When to use it: Use it for travel, events or countdowns, to change places, times or numbers with a mechanical feel.
