Motion Prompt #257

What it is: Bends what is behind it like a lens of water
What the sample shows: A rounded glass bar glides back and forth over a colorful heading. Inside it, the colors and letters underneath swell and bend more toward the edges; a white highlight runs along the top edge.
Spec:
- A 511 × 170 viewport. The background is a blue → mid blue gradient (#2F6BEB → #9DB8F6) with three blurred circles (light blue, white, pale blue; 22 px blur) and the heading LIGHT / IN MOTION. One pass takes 4.7 s (twice per 9.4 s pack cycle).
- Bar 176 × 58 (radius 29), centered at x = 255 + 140·sin(2πt/4.7), y = 64 + 16·sin(4πt/4.7).
- Bending: the background is redrawn magnified around the bar's center: 1.7× in the outer ring, 1.38× from 7 px in and 1.22× from 13 px in, so it swells more toward the edges (standing in for refraction).
- Finish: a 10% white film, white lines along the top and bottom edges (gradient opacity 0.95 and 0.6), a thin mid-blue inner line and an outer shadow (blur 18, 6 down). Three icons inside.
- Unlike glassmorphism (frosted blur), it does not blur: it bends like a lens. It is also the name of the design language Apple announced in 2025.
When to use it: Use it for navigation, tabs or buttons that float over photos and color, to make them feel light and current.
