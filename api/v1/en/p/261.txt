Motion Prompt #261

What it is: Leaf shadows and sunlight sway on a wall
What the sample shows: Slanted white window light falls on a pale gray to pale blue wall and blurred leaf shadows sway slowly across it, with a heading and a vase in front.
Spec:
- A 511 × 170 viewport; the wall goes #F1F0EE → #F1F5FF. One pass takes 4.7 s (twice per 9.4 s pack cycle).
- Sunlight: a trapezoid (top edge 170–470, bottom edge 60–380) filled with a white gradient (opacity 0–0.95) in screen.
- Leaf shadows: three branches (230 long) with five or six pairs of leaves (17 × 7 ellipses). Branches sway ±0.06 rad and leaves ±0.22 rad, at 2πt/4.7 and twice that, each with its own phase.
- The shadows are drawn offscreen in blue (#2F6BEB), blurred 4.5 px and laid over at 0.3 opacity. Blue rather than gray shadows keep a bright palette looking sunlit.
- With photos, a PNG or video of real branches can be laid over instead.
When to use it: Use it behind ceramics, furniture, skincare or cafe sites that should feel calm and naturally lit.
